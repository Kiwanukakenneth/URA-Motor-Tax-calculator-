HOW URA CALCULATES YOUR TAXES

All taxes are levied under the East African Community Common External Tariff (EAC-CET) and collected by the Uganda Revenue Authority (URA). Tax rates verified against real URA output data.

WHAT IS CIF?

CIF = Cost + Insurance + Freight. URA taxes your vehicle on its CIF value. If your purchase price is lower than URA's Motor Vehicle Value Guide, URA will use their value — not yours.
