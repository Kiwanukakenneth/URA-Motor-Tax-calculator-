HOW URA CALCULATES YOUR TAXES

All taxes are levied under the East African Community Common External Tariff (EAC-CET), the Uganda Revenue Authority Act, and the Value Added Tax Act Cap 349. URA assesses taxes on the higher of your purchase price or their Motor Vehicle Value Guide CIF.

WHAT IS CIF?

CIF = Cost + Insurance + Freight. This is the standard international trade valuation method. URA taxes your vehicle on its CIF value — which includes the purchase price, shipping and insurance costs to the Uganda border.

⚠️ URA Override Rule: If your purchase price is lower than URA's Motor Vehicle Value Guide, URA will use their value — not yours. This prevents undervaluation at customs.
